A tribute in five chapters

Lewis Hamilton

From a karting track in Hertfordshire to seven world titles. An unofficial look at one of the most successful careers Formula 1 has seen.

Scroll to race
The story

A kid from Stevenage who raced on second-hand karts, told the boss of a top team he wanted to drive for them one day, and then spent two decades rewriting the record books. Seven championships. More wins and more pole positions than anyone before him. And a voice that kept pushing the sport to look like the world that watches it.

Career

Five chapters

Scroll and the season rolls by. Each chapter is a turning point, from the first kart to the switch that surprised the whole paddock.

Chapter 01 · The kart

The foundation

Born in Stevenage in 1985, he started karting with his father, who worked several jobs to keep him racing. At ten he met a team boss at an awards night and told him he would drive for him one day.

Chapter 02 · The debut

Rookie on fire

A podium on his Formula 1 debut and a first win in Canada later that season. He missed the title by a single point as a rookie, and the whole sport took notice.

Chapter 03 · The first crown

Champion at 23

Decided on the final corners of the final race in Brazil, his first world championship made him the youngest champion the sport had seen at the time.

Chapter 04 · The dynasty

Six more titles

A move in 2013 became an era. Titles in 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020 took him to seven, alongside the most wins and poles in the history of the sport.

Chapter 05 · The new red

A new chapter

In 2025 he began a fresh adventure in red: one of the biggest driver moves in Formula 1 history, chasing more history with the sport’s most famous team.

Signature circuits

Where he ruled

Great Britain

Silverstone

Home. More British Grand Prix wins than any driver in history, in front of the loudest crowd on the calendar.

Hungary

Hungaroring

A twisting, dusty circuit where overtaking is hard, and where he won more often than at any other track.

Canada

Montréal

Where it all clicked: the first Grand Prix win of his career came here in his rookie season.

Brazil

Interlagos

The rain, the last lap, the fifth place he needed. The first title was won here in one of the most dramatic finishes ever.

Abu Dhabi

Yas Marina

The twilight finale. Several of his championship seasons ended under its lights, and some of his most famous duels did too.

Beyond the track

More than lap times

A knighthood

Knighted in 2021 for services to motorsport, he became Sir Lewis, while still racing at the front.

Opening doors

He set up a commission and a charitable foundation to help young people from under-represented backgrounds into motorsport, engineering and science.

A voice in the paddock

He used his platform to speak about diversity, equality and the environment, and pushed the sport to change with him.

Style and culture

Music, fashion and film: few drivers have carried Formula 1 so far beyond the racing world.
